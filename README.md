# You Rang?

A tiny bell page: tap the bell, and the butler's phone gets a notification through [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh). He taps a reply button and the reply comes back as a notification.

The page needs its notification channel names in the link, after `#`: `index.html#k=BUTLER_CHANNEL&c=REPLY_CHANNEL`. Open the page without them to generate a fresh pair. Keep your link private.
